Detection modules

Three independent readings, one answer

An attacker who beats the face model still has to beat the voice model and the language model in the same clip. Each pipeline runs on its own evidence, so their agreement is what makes a verdict hard to fake.

Face

MediaPipe crops a face from every frame and EfficientNet-B4, fine-tuned on FaceForensics++ and Celeb-DF-v2, hunts for GAN artefacts, blending seams, and texture that does not belong. AUC-ROC above 0.95.

EfficientNet-B4MediaPipeFaceForensics++Celeb-DF-v2

Voice

Log-Mel spectrograms and MFCCs feed AASIST, a graph attention network fine-tuned on ASVspoof 2021. It catches text-to-speech, voice conversion, and neural vocoder residue at under 5% equal error rate.

AASISTASVspoof 2021MFCCLog-Mel

Language

BERT fine-tuned on HC3 separates machine-written speech from human speech. When the transcript is thin or the service is offline, a statistical fallback scores filler density, bigram entropy, and type-token ratio.

BERTHC3Bigram entropyTF-IDF

Fusion engine

A trained MLP weighs the three scores, lifts anything that looks anomalous against the others, and returns a calibrated probability with a confidence interval, an alert severity, and plain-language evidence for every point it deducted. Results stream to the dashboard over a WebSocket as they land.

How it works

Upload to verdict, start to finish

Step 1

Drop the media in

A video, an audio file, or pasted text. Drag and drop works anywhere on the upload panel.

Step 2

Three models run at once

FastAPI fans the job out to the face, voice, and language pipelines in parallel.

Step 3

Scores are fused

The MLP applies learned weights and lifts anything one model flags that the others missed.

Step 4

You get a verdict and a reason

Calibrated score, confidence interval, severity, and the evidence behind each deduction.
