Detection Modules

Three pipelines.
One verdict.

Each module targets a different attack surface. Together they make DeepShield adversarially robust.

01 / Face Detection

EfficientNet-B4 Visual Analysis

MediaPipe extracts face crops from every frame. EfficientNet-B4 fine-tuned on FaceForensics++ and Celeb-DF-v2 detects GAN artifacts, blending boundaries, and texture anomalies with AUC-ROC above 0.95.

EfficientNet-B4 MediaPipe FaceForensics++ Celeb-DF-v2
02 / Voice Analysis

AASIST Voice Spoof Detection

Log-Mel spectrograms and MFCCs feed the AASIST graph attention network fine-tuned on ASVspoof 2021. Detects TTS synthesis, voice conversion, and neural vocoder artifacts with EER below 5%.

AASIST ASVspoof 2021 MFCC Log-Mel
03 / Linguistic NLP

BERT Linguistic Pattern Analysis

Fine-tuned BERT on HC3 dataset classifies AI-generated vs human text. Statistical fallback measures filler word density, bigram entropy, type-token ratio, and passive voice ratio for offline analysis.

BERT HC3 Dataset Bigram Entropy TF-IDF
04 / Risk Fusion

MLP Fusion Engine with XAI

A trained MLP combines Face (35%), Voice (35%), and NLP (30%) scores with anomaly boosting. Outputs a calibrated authenticity probability, confidence intervals, alert severity, and XAI evidence bullets.

MLP Fusion XAI Confidence Intervals WebSocket
